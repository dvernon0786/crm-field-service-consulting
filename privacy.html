Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-08-24.

What we collect

When you submit the quote form, we collect the organization name, your role, team size range, current system (if provided), and email address you enter. We also log the page you submitted from.

How we use it

Solely to prepare and send the pricing information you requested. We do not sell or share this information with third parties for marketing purposes.

How long we keep it

Submission records are retained for as long as needed to respond to your request and for a reasonable follow-up period, after which they are deleted.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate page traffic. No individually identifying advertising cookies are set.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any information you've submitted by contacting us through the quote form's reply channel.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the quote form.
