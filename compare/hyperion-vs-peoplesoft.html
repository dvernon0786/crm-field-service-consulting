Hyperion vs PeopleSoft: field service software comparison

Side-by-side analysis for field service teams evaluating these two platforms. Features, cost, and a stated recommendation.

Verdict: Hyperion for organisations in Financial Services, Manufacturing, Energy that need best-in-class financial consolidation and planning. PeopleSoft is the stronger choice for Government, Higher Education, Healthcare where mature hr/payroll matters most.

Where they differ for field service

Hyperion strengths: Best-in-class financial consolidation and planning; deep scenario modelling.

Hyperion limitations: Legacy on-premise; Oracle pushing to EPM Cloud; high maintenance cost.

PeopleSoft strengths: Mature HR/payroll; large installed base in government and higher-ed; deep localisation.

PeopleSoft limitations: Legacy on-premise architecture; high upgrade cost; Oracle pushing migration to Fusion.

Choose Hyperion if

  • Your organisation fits the Enterprise (500+ employees) profile
  • You operate in Financial Services, Manufacturing, Energy
  • Your integration requirements align with FDMEE/Data Integration; REST APIs for EPM Cloud
  • Your implementation budget has room for the $200,000–$2,000,000 range
  • You need compliance coverage for SOX, ASC 606

Choose PeopleSoft if

  • Your organisation fits the Enterprise (1000+ employees) profile
  • You operate in Government, Higher Education, Healthcare
  • Your integration requirements align with PeopleSoft Integration Broker; REST/SOAP; OIC adapters
  • Your implementation budget fits the $400,000–$4,000,000 range
  • You need compliance coverage for SOX, HIPAA, GDPR

Frequently asked questions

Which is better for field service: Hyperion or PeopleSoft?

Hyperion for organisations in Financial Services, Manufacturing, Energy that need best-in-class financial consolidation and planning. PeopleSoft is the stronger choice for Government, Higher Education, Healthcare where mature hr/payroll matters most.

How do Hyperion and PeopleSoft compare on implementation cost?

Hyperion implementation typically costs $200,000–$2,000,000 over 9–24 months. PeopleSoft typically costs $400,000–$4,000,000 over 12–36 months. Implementation services are the larger cost in year 1 for both.
