Where they differ for field service

Hyperion strengths: Best-in-class financial consolidation and planning; deep scenario modelling.

Hyperion limitations: Legacy on-premise; Oracle pushing to EPM Cloud; high maintenance cost.

Workday strengths: Modern UI; strong HR and workforce planning; real-time reporting with Prism Analytics.

Workday limitations: Financial Management less mature than Oracle/SAP; high cost; limited manufacturing.

Choose Hyperion if

  • Your organisation fits the Enterprise (500+ employees) profile
  • You operate in Financial Services, Manufacturing, Energy
  • Your integration requirements align with FDMEE/Data Integration; REST APIs for EPM Cloud
  • Your implementation budget has room for the $200,000–$2,000,000 range
  • You need compliance coverage for SOX, ASC 606

Choose Workday if

  • Your organisation fits the Mid-market to Enterprise (500+ employees) profile
  • You operate in Technology, Healthcare, Financial Services, Higher Education
  • Your integration requirements align with Workday Integration Cloud; REST APIs; pre-built connectors for Salesforce/ADP/ServiceNow
  • Your implementation budget fits the $300,000–$3,000,000 range
  • You need compliance coverage for SOX, HIPAA, GDPR
