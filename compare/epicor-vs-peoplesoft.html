Epicor vs PeopleSoft: field service software comparison

Side-by-side analysis for field service teams evaluating these two platforms. Features, cost, and a stated recommendation.

Verdict: Epicor for most field service teams — lower entry cost ($80,000 vs $400,000 implementation floor) and faster time-to-value. PeopleSoft makes sense for larger organisations in Government, Higher Education, Healthcare where its deeper capabilities justify the cost.

Where they differ for field service

Epicor strengths: Manufacturing-first ERP; strong shop floor and MES integration; industry-specific modules.

Epicor limitations: Smaller ecosystem; UI dated in on-premise version; limited global multi-entity.

PeopleSoft strengths: Mature HR/payroll; large installed base in government and higher-ed; deep localisation.

PeopleSoft limitations: Legacy on-premise architecture; high upgrade cost; Oracle pushing migration to Fusion.

Choose Epicor if

  • Your organisation fits the SMB to Mid-market (50–1000 employees) profile
  • You operate in Manufacturing, Distribution, Automotive
  • Your integration requirements align with Epicor Integration Agent; REST APIs; EDI connectors
  • Your implementation budget has room for the $80,000–$800,000 range
  • You need compliance coverage for SOX (partial), GDPR

Choose PeopleSoft if

  • Your organisation fits the Enterprise (1000+ employees) profile
  • You operate in Government, Higher Education, Healthcare
  • Your integration requirements align with PeopleSoft Integration Broker; REST/SOAP; OIC adapters
  • Your implementation budget fits the $400,000–$4,000,000 range
  • You need compliance coverage for SOX, HIPAA, GDPR

Frequently asked questions

Which is better for field service: Epicor or PeopleSoft?

Epicor for most field service teams — lower entry cost ($80,000 vs $400,000 implementation floor) and faster time-to-value. PeopleSoft makes sense for larger organisations in Government, Higher Education, Healthcare where its deeper capabilities justify the cost.

How do Epicor and PeopleSoft compare on implementation cost?

Epicor implementation typically costs $80,000–$800,000 over 6–18 months. PeopleSoft typically costs $400,000–$4,000,000 over 12–36 months. Implementation services are the larger cost in year 1 for both.
