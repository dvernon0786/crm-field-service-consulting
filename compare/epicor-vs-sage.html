Epicor vs Sage: field service software comparison

Side-by-side analysis for field service teams evaluating these two platforms. Features, cost, and a stated recommendation.

Verdict: Sage for most field service teams — lower entry cost ($25,000 vs $80,000 implementation floor) and faster time-to-value. Epicor makes sense for larger organisations in Manufacturing, Distribution, Automotive where its deeper capabilities justify the cost.

Where they differ for field service

Epicor strengths: Manufacturing-first ERP; strong shop floor and MES integration; industry-specific modules.

Epicor limitations: Smaller ecosystem; UI dated in on-premise version; limited global multi-entity.

Sage strengths: Strong financials (Intacct); good for multi-entity non-profits; Sage X3 solid for manufacturing.

Sage limitations: Smaller ecosystem; Sage X3 on-premise support declining; limited global reach.

Choose Epicor if

  • Your organisation fits the SMB to Mid-market (50–1000 employees) profile
  • You operate in Manufacturing, Distribution, Automotive
  • Your integration requirements align with Epicor Integration Agent; REST APIs; EDI connectors
  • Your implementation budget has room for the $80,000–$800,000 range
  • You need compliance coverage for SOX (partial), GDPR

Choose Sage if

  • Your organisation fits the SMB to Mid-market (10–500 employees) profile
  • You operate in Nonprofit, Manufacturing, Professional Services
  • Your integration requirements align with Sage API; pre-built connectors for Salesforce/ADP
  • Your implementation budget fits the $25,000–$400,000 range
  • You need compliance coverage for SOX (partial), HIPAA (Intacct), ASC 606

Frequently asked questions

Which is better for field service: Epicor or Sage?

Sage for most field service teams — lower entry cost ($25,000 vs $80,000 implementation floor) and faster time-to-value. Epicor makes sense for larger organisations in Manufacturing, Distribution, Automotive where its deeper capabilities justify the cost.

How do Epicor and Sage compare on implementation cost?

Epicor implementation typically costs $80,000–$800,000 over 6–18 months. Sage typically costs $25,000–$400,000 over 3–12 months. Implementation services are the larger cost in year 1 for both.
