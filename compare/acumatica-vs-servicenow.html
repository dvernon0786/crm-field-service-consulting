Where they differ for field service

Acumatica strengths: No per-user fees (consumption pricing); strong construction and distribution modules.

Acumatica limitations: Smaller partner ecosystem than SAP/Oracle; reporting less mature; limited large-enterprise track record.

ServiceNow strengths: Best-in-class workflow automation; IT asset management; strong field service module.

ServiceNow limitations: Not a full-stack ERP (no native manufacturing or inventory); financial module is thin.

Choose Acumatica if

  • Your organisation fits the SMB to Mid-market (5–500 employees) profile
  • You operate in Construction, Distribution, Manufacturing, Retail
  • Your integration requirements align with Open APIs; REST; pre-built Salesforce/Shopify connectors
  • Your implementation budget has room for the $30,000–$300,000 range
  • You need compliance coverage for GDPR, SOX (partial)

Choose ServiceNow if

  • Your organisation fits the Mid-market to Enterprise (200+ employees) profile
  • You operate in Technology, Healthcare, Government
  • Your integration requirements align with Integration Hub; REST/SOAP; MID Server for on-premise data
  • Your implementation budget fits the $150,000–$2,000,000 range
  • You need compliance coverage for SOX, HIPAA, GDPR

Frequently asked questions

Which is better for field service: Acumatica or ServiceNow?

Acumatica for most field service teams — lower entry cost ($30,000 vs $150,000 implementation floor) and faster time-to-value. ServiceNow makes sense for larger organisations in Technology, Healthcare, Government where its deeper capabilities justify the cost.

How do Acumatica and ServiceNow compare on implementation cost?

Acumatica implementation typically costs $30,000–$300,000 over 3–9 months. ServiceNow typically costs $150,000–$2,000,000 over 6–18 months. Implementation services are the larger cost in year 1 for both.
