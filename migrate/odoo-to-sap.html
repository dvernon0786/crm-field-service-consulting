Odoo to SAP migration: field service data and workflow

What moving from Odoo to SAP actually involves for field service teams — data mapping, timeline, effort range, and the hardest part.

Why teams move from Odoo

Odoo teams typically move when they outgrow the platform's enterprise support capacity, need stronger multi-entity financial reporting, or require compliance modules (SOX, HIPAA) that Odoo does not cover natively.

What you're moving to: SAP

SAP S/4HANA is a ERP platform from SAP SE, best suited for Mid-market to Enterprise (500+ employees) organisations in Manufacturing, Oil and Gas, Utilities, Logistics. Strengths relevant to this migration: Deep manufacturing and supply chain; mature compliance tooling; global multi-entity support. Known limitations to factor in: High TCO; long implementation; requires dedicated SAP BASIS team; heavy customisation lock-in.

The hardest part of this migration

Data extraction from Odoo: Odoo's open-source database is PostgreSQL and extractable, but field service data quality varies widely depending on how customised the instance is. Heavily modified Odoo installations may have non-standard field mappings that require manual schema review before migration.

This is the step that consistently takes longer than planned. Budget time for a data audit before any tooling decisions — the quality and structure of your Odoo data determines whether the migration runs in months or quarters.

Migration sequence

  1. Data audit (weeks 1–3). Extract a sample of Odoo records. Assess completeness, duplicates, and schema gaps. This is where the effort estimate gets refined — everything that follows depends on data quality.
  2. Target system configuration (weeks 2–8, parallel). Configure SAP before any data arrives: work order types, skill definitions, SLA rules, dispatch board layout, and mobile app settings. Data migration into an unconfigured system creates rework.
  3. Historical data migration (weeks 6–14). Migrate closed work orders, customer records, asset register, and technician profiles. Validate record counts and spot-check a sample of complex records.
  4. Integration cutover (weeks 10–16). Connect SAP to your billing system, ERP, and any other adjacent tools. Test the full job lifecycle — intake to invoice — end to end before go-live.
  5. Parallel run (4–8 weeks). Dispatch from SAP. Maintain Odoo as a read-only reference. Reconcile daily. This is the phase that teams consistently underestimate — budget the dispatcher time to run both systems simultaneously.
  6. Cutover. Freeze Odoo data. Complete delta migration of records created during parallel run. Go live. Retain Odoo read-only access for 90 days minimum.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a Odoo to SAP migration take?

12–36 months from kickoff to cutover, including parallel run. The variable is data complexity and integration count. Clean, well-structured Odoo data migrates faster than heavily customised instances with years of accumulated work order history.

What is the hardest part of migrating from Odoo to SAP?

Odoo's open-source database is PostgreSQL and extractable, but field service data quality varies widely depending on how customised the instance is. Heavily modified Odoo installations may have non-standard field mappings that require manual schema review before migration. Beyond the technical extraction, the hardest operational challenge is the parallel run — running two dispatch systems simultaneously while keeping records reconciled.

Can we migrate open work orders from Odoo to SAP?

In-flight work orders at cutover are the most difficult records to migrate cleanly. Most teams handle these manually: freeze Odoo at cutover, complete a snapshot of open orders, and create them fresh in SAP. Automated migration of open work orders with live technician assignments rarely works without significant reconciliation effort.
